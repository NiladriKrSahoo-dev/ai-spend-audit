# User Interviews — Vantage

## Status: Outreach Attempted (0 Responses)

I am being 100% transparent with the Credex team: I do not have three completed interview transcripts for this submission. 
---
Over the weekend, I initiated cold outreach to 6 potential users (Seed-stage founders and Lead Engineers) via X, LinkedIn, Instagram and Slack. I pitched Vantage as a solution to their AI billing "shock." 

### The Result:
As of my submission deadline, I have received 0 responses. 
---
I learned a hard lesson about the "Cold Start" problem in entrepreneurship: **Timing and Volume are everything.** 1. **Timing:** Reaching out over the weekend to busy CTOs was a "mid" move. They are either offline or clearing their inbox for Monday morning, and a DM from a new builder easily gets buried.
2. **Volume:** 6 outreaches isn't enough. In a real GTM motion, I should have hit 50+ people to secure 3 meetings.
3. **Execution Gap:** I spent 90% of my week locked in on the Audit Engine logic and the Redis persistence layer. I treated this like a technical challenge when it’s actually a distribution challenge.

### What I would have asked:
Even without the calls, I developed these high-intent questions to validate my "Ghost Seat" hypothesis:
1. "When you see your monthly Cursor or Claude bill, do you actually know which seats are active and which are 'ghosts'?"
2. "If I could show you exactly where you're losing $500/mo in 30 seconds, would you actually act on it, or is that just 'the cost of doing business' for you?"
3. "Is the biggest pain the actual dollar amount, or the time it takes to manually audit 5 different AI dashboards?"

I am still committed to these conversations. Even after I submit this, I’m going to keep pushing for those replies because I want to know if the product I built actually solves a Tier-1 problem.
