# Metrics — Vantage

## North Star: "Lead Conversion Rate"
The % of people who run an audit and actually submit their email for the report. If this number is high, it means the perceived value of the AI summary is worth the user's data.

## Input Metrics
1. **Tool Depth:** Average number of tools added per audit (Goal: > 3).
2. **Share Rate:** % of users who click "Copy Link" for their shareable report.
3. **Resend Open Rate:** % of users who open the follow-up email.

## Pivot Decision
If the **Lead Conversion Rate is < 5%**, the AI summary isn't valuable enough. I’d pivot the product into a browser extension that automatically catches waste while you're inside the Stripe billing dashboard.
